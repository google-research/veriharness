# Falsification — try to break what they share

Wherever the rollouts agree, their agreement is not evidence: they share a model and its blind
spots, so they are consistently right or consistently wrong and the agreement itself cannot
tell you which. Your job is to find the positions they hold in common and try to break them,
and to leave a record of every attempt. Where they disagree, leave it — another session is
working those. Read `fals/LEDGER.md` now.

Nothing is rebuilt on the strength of a suspicion. An attempt that ends in doubt buys nothing:
carry it to a result, or record that you could not.

- Start with the delivery contract, because it is cheap and a whole pool often misses it
  together: the file names, types, count and location the spec names; any stated length,
  format or "preserve the original" instruction. `ls` every bundle against the spec's list.
- Recompute the load-bearing figures from the raw inputs in workspace/ — not from a candidate's
  intermediate table. The two shapes that most often break a consensus are a figure that does
  not reproduce, and a citation, id or file reference that turns out not to exist.
- Challenge the shared reading, not just the shared numbers. They may agree only because they
  all took the same reading of an underdetermined spec. Ask what else it could defensibly mean,
  then look in workspace/ for evidence of which was intended — the file the task names, the
  figure the source states, the label the workbook carries. Where the materials supply an
  answer, a re-derivation that departs from it is the shared error.
- Unanimous omission is also consensus. Check spec/ for obligations no rollout met.
- Before you spend a check, ask whether the task asked for this at all. A flaw carried in from
  the input that the task never asked to fix, or a figure the brief never requested, is not a
  broken consensus however real it is.

Spend your effort where a break would be worth the most: a claim the whole deliverable rests
on is worth three checks; a detail is worth none.

# Endgame

Write `ledger_fals.json` in the task root, in the format `fals/LEDGER.md` gives. Do not decide
and do not pick a candidate; the decision is made by a separate session from your record.
